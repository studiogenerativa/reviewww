Reviewww. Comment. Improve.
Now on the Chrome Web Store

Turn a walkthrough of your site into a to-do list your coding agent can run.

Click any element on any page, including localhost, and leave a comment. Reviewww pins it to that element and saves the route, the viewport, a CSS selector and a screenshot, so nobody has to guess what “that button” meant.

Add to Chrome Free. No account, no server.

How it works

  1. Start a reviewww on the site you are looking at and add the people reviewing with you.
  2. Press C to comment, V to browse normally, H to hide the pins.
  3. Reply in threads and move comments between Unresolved, Active and Completed.
  4. Open the extension to see every comment by page and status, with its screenshot.
  5. Export as a prompt: a structured brief for Claude Code, Cursor, Codex or a teammate.

Built for real reviews

  • Works on any site and on local development servers.
  • Several reviewers on the same screen, each with their own color.
  • Finish the session when the meeting ends; fix things later from your desk.
  • If the page changes and an element moves, re-pin the comment in one click.

Private by design

No account, no server, no analytics. Everything you create stays in your browser, on your device. We never receive it.
